CRICPRO

A motion sensor on the bat records every swing. CricPro turns the readings into bat speed, impact and shot direction.

Bat speed Peak speed of each shot in km/h, worked out from how fast the bat turns.
Impact and control Peak impact in g, a control score out of 100 and an accuracy percentage.
Shot direction Each shot is marked leg side, off side or straight.
Sensor and browser The sensor connects over Bluetooth in Chrome or Edge. A built-in demo works without it.
